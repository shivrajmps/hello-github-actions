# Script: Budget = 3 Numbers. 50, 30, 20. Bas.

## 0:00 HOOK
[ON SCREEN: Excel sheet open, 40 rows, cursor blinking. You slam the laptop shut.]
Stop opening Excel. Your whole budget is 3 numbers. 50, 30 and 20. Aur yeh video khatam hone se pehle tumhare paas woh teen numbers honge, tumhari salary ke saath.

## 0:15 THE TURN
[ON SCREEN: Calendar. 1st tareekh pe "SALARY" green mein. 20th pe "₹0" red mein.]
Aaj main sirf ek cheez karunga: tumhari salary ko teen dabbon mein daalunga, aur dikhaunga ki paisa 20 tareekh ko kyun gayab hota hai. Shuru.

## 0:30 BEAT 1 - The crime scene
[ON SCREEN: Three empty boxes labelled NEEDS, WANTS, FUTURE. Sabse upar "SALARY" ka arrow.]
Salary ek hi hai, lekin usko kheenchne wale teen log hain. Pehla: NEEDS. Rent, bijli, ration, EMI, metro. Yeh woh bill hain jo tum na do toh landlord khud aa jaata hai.
[ON SCREEN: NEEDS box fills to 50%.]
Rule kehta hai: salary ka 50 percent, maximum, yahan. Agar tumhara rent plus EMI pehle hi 50 se upar hai, toh problem budget nahi hai, problem flat hai. Woh alag video.

## 1:05 BEAT 2 - The thief who smiles
[ON SCREEN: Zomato notification, Swiggy notification, "Sale ends tonight". Teen notifications ek ke baad ek.]
Doosra: WANTS. Bahar ka khaana, OTT, naye shoes, weekend. Yeh woh hai jisse tum "treat" bolte ho.
[ON SCREEN: WANTS box fills to 30%, then overflows and spills into the FUTURE box.]
Rule: 30 percent. Aur yahan sach bolte hain: 20 tareekh ko paisa isliye khatam hota hai kyunki yeh dabba 30 pe nahi rukta. Yeh teesre dabbe ko kha jaata hai. Aur teesra dabba chup rehta hai, kyunki uska koi notification nahi aata.

## 1:45 BEAT 3 - The one nobody feeds
[ON SCREEN: FUTURE box, empty, dust blowing. Caption: "last fed: never".]
Teesra: FUTURE. Savings, investment, emergency fund. Rule: 20 percent.
[ON SCREEN: Salary day. Arrow from SALARY straight into FUTURE box FIRST, before anything else.]
Aur yahan ek trick hai jo poore rule ko kaam karne deti hai. Yeh 20 percent mahine ke END mein nahi bachta. Yeh salary aane ke DIN nikal jaata hai. Pehle. Rent se bhi pehle. Kyunki jo paisa account mein dikhta hai, woh kharch hota hai. Yeh maths nahi hai, yeh insaan hai.

## 2:25 BEAT 4 - Do it with me, now
[ON SCREEN: Phone calculator, big digits. Blank line: "Salary: ______".]
Ab phone uthao. Apni salary type karo. Main apni nahi bataunga, tum apni daalo.
[ON SCREEN: Calculator: salary × 0.5 → NEEDS. salary × 0.3 → WANTS. salary × 0.2 → FUTURE.]
Point five se multiply. Yeh NEEDS ki limit. Point three se. Yeh WANTS ki limit. Point two se. Yeh woh amount hai jo salary ke din account se nikalke alag jaata hai. Teen numbers. Screenshot le lo. Yahi tumhara budget hai.

## 3:00 BEAT 5 - The honest part
[ON SCREEN: Text: "50/30/20 is a starting point, not a law."]
Ek baat saaf: yeh rule perfect nahi hai. Metro city mein rent akela 50 kha jaata hai. Toh 60-25-15 karo, 70-20-10 karo. Numbers badlo, order mat badlo. FUTURE pehle, NEEDS doosra, WANTS jo bach jaaye.

## 3:20 PAYOFF
[ON SCREEN: The three boxes, filled correctly. Calendar: 20th tareekh pe ab "₹0" nahi, FUTURE box green.]
Toh promise tha teen numbers, video khatam hone se pehle. Woh teen numbers tumhare calculator pe abhi hain. Yahi woh poora budget hai jiske liye log 40-row Excel banate hain. Teen numbers. Bas.

## 3:35 CLOSE
[ON SCREEN: Comment box animation. Text: "Tumhara ratio?"]
Ek kaam karo. Neeche comment mein apna ratio likho. 50-30-20, ya jo bhi tumhare rent ne tumhe banane pe majboor kiya. Agle video mein sabse common wale ko pakad ke theek karunga.
